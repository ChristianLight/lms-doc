# 05. Delivery, scheduling and exam access

One AssessmentDelivery references a published AssessmentVersion, one CourseOffering and one ContentPlacement in an eligible CourseVersion. Record the delivery's stable identity independently of the reusable AssessmentVersion. Each published DeliveryPolicyVersion pins release schedules, candidate audience, access requirements, attempt quotas, navigation, timing, submission, expiry, accommodations and review visibility. Schedule changes create a new policy version. Existing attempts retain their policy snapshots until an authorized targeted adjustment.

## Purpose and defaults

| Purpose | Baseline configuration | Grade path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice | Unlimited attempts, immediate safe feedback, untimed, self-paced, no review queue by default | Progress or mastery evidence only unless delivery explicitly uses a grade item |
| Survey | Untimed, one response by default, feedback optional | Completion signal only. Anonymous mode uses separated evidence and capability redemption |
| Coursework | One submit/resubmit cycle unless staff grant more, due and hard close separate, late submission permitted between due and close, staff marks if required | GradeEvidence after accepted marking |
| Quiz | One attempt by default, randomized order optional, exact duration if set, controlled feedback after finish | GradeEvidence for each accepted attempt. Academic policy chooses the result |
| Formal exam | One attempt, explicit availability window, server duration, no writable grace, immediate private receipt and delayed feedback | Accepted marking and separate controlled disclosure |

Every candidate needs offering access, a current EnrollmentRelease for the exact delivery placement CourseVersion at start, an audience match and any course prerequisites or progress gates. An examiner may require identity verification or supervised release through a scoped ExamAccessGrant or registered external approval. A short exam passcode can supplement enrollment checks, with a stored salted verifier and independent attempt rate limit. It does not replace authentication. Exam staff cannot waive access requirements they lack permission to administer. Material delivery and grade review separately check authorization for former learners after withdrawal.

## Time model

Store UTC instants at declared precision plus source time zone and original local notation for schedules. Require a zone ID and an explicit offset choice for ambiguous daylight-saving input. Reject nonexistent local times until corrected. The effective window is `[opens_at, closes_at)`. The candidate start and every response mutation must be admitted strictly before the hard deadline. Due time is a late classification boundary, with `sealed_at >= due_at` marked late unless an allowed override applies. Course deadline and sitting deadline are independent. With a non-null close, the final writable instant is the earliest of close, start plus effective duration and any earlier section limit. A candidate may finish an active section after a section deadline only if its published policy locks that section and advances to another within the whole assessment window.

Default effective exam duration is an integer seconds value after policy resolution. Duration policy can replace seconds, add seconds or multiply by a rational approved extension factor. Reject conflicting kinds within the same effective precedence. Round a fractional extension up to the next second. Hard close still caps the deadline unless an accommodation overrides close. Warn staff when an extra-time rule cannot take effect because of hard close. Publish planned duration and effective timing to the candidate before start.

At a lock-protected operation, obtain one authoritative server UTC decision time. Do not use device time, ingress time or last seen clock as evidence of admission. A durable last-decision timestamp per sitting rejects backward adjustments for authorization. A clock outage pauses creation of new timed attempts. A significant clock anomaly opens a staff incident and preserves evidence. Record scheduled auto-finalization effective time at the deadline and actual processing time separately. A worker running later cannot admit additional answers.

A scored submission delay is calculated from the candidate's last admitted scored submission in the same delivery. While it runs, a newly eligible draft can remain writable. A scored submit returns the next eligible instant until that instant is reached. A compatible retain-first-form policy captures the original realized display and scoring inputs in the new attempt while the new attempt keeps its own responses and receipt.

### Worked timings

A delivery opens at 08:00, closes at 10:00 and permits 90 minutes. A student starting at 08:40 has an effective hard deadline of 10:00. A group gets 30% extra time. Its new duration is 117 minutes. A learner starting at 08:40 still gets 10:00 unless a close extension exists. A learner-specific close of 11:00 gives that learner 10:37. An autosave admitted at 10:36:59.900 is eligible. A save admitted at 10:37:00.000 is rejected. If the expiry worker wakes at 10:38, it seals the last acknowledged eligible revision and records both the scheduled and actual finalization times.

An exam begun at 09:00 has a base duration 60 minutes and approved pauses up to 10 minutes total. A pause from 09:15 to 09:22 consumes seven minutes and gives a duration deadline of 10:07, capped by close. A resume exactly at a hard close has no effect. A pause never permits answer edits. Disable optional pause unless the delivery policy defines count, total seconds, eligible actors and a separate condition for freezing or hiding the candidate form.

## Accommodation resolution

Each AssessmentOverride names delivery and an Enrollment, Section or LearningGroup scope. It contains a validity interval, revision, grant reason code, grantor, approval and explicitly allowed field-level changes. Read protected support details through a separate permission and store only effective accessibility and time settings in the attempt. Process one approved applicable override per field at the highest precedence. Individual enrollment outranks section and learning group, which outrank delivery defaults. Reject overlapping same-priority field changes if both scopes can apply and values differ. A named priority model can resolve a known institutional exception if published before the exam. Independent fields merge, so a group alternate presentation and an individual time extension can both apply.

Resolve at start using effective membership at that instant. Freeze the result plus provenance of each applied field. A change to group membership or policy does not change the sitting. Staff may issue an approved `AdjustAttemptTiming` command for an active sitting with a reason and candidate notification. Require the current attempt version and exact replacement deadline or delta. Ordinary staff adjustment can extend only. A correction that shortens an active deadline requires incident authority, candidate notice and a future minimum notice interval. An expired or sealed attempt cannot be reopened. Remediation grants a new linked attempt or accepts existing evidence through review. Never overwrite the original effective policy or reset an ordinal.

## Candidate and staff workflows

Candidates see an availability screen with policy, allowed materials, effective dates and duration, accommodations actually applied, device/accessibility readiness check and a start confirmation. Start button state distinguishes not-open, denied, can-resume, active elsewhere, limit-exceeded, suspended and closed. It reveals only the candidate's own reason. The sitting UI shows server-relative remaining time and warns by default at five minutes, one minute and fifteen seconds without stealing focus. For flexible tasks, allow the configured extension or time-limit removal. For essential timing, retain documented authorized alternatives. The staff delivery dashboard shows schedules and version changes, accommodation coverage, sessions by state, outage incidents and permitted actions without exposing candidate answers to access-only supervisors.

A proctoring capability is optional and has separate consent, data minimization, retention and access policies. A proctoring result supplies incident evidence for independent review. It does not automatically change a score. An `ExamIncident` can reference a delivery or attempt and evidence files. An independent reviewer claims the incident and records no action, approved extension, approved retake, approved void or escalation. An authorized follow-up command must enforce the exact disposition under the target attempt lock. Accessibility target [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#timing-adjustable) and explicit timing exceptions apply to candidate flows.

## Policy state and operational gates

Draft delivery is invisible to learners. Publication verifies offering relationship, candidate roles, policy schema, accessible renderers, supported scorer builds, active item dependencies, event policies and grade bindings. Suspension blocks new starts and records a policy for existing attempts. Default suspension lets existing eligible sessions proceed and surfaces an alert to staff. A safety suspension uses the incident workflow for each affected active attempt. Apply an approved disposition without deleting acknowledged responses. Closing a delivery blocks new starts and preserves reviews. After closure, the retention owner may archive the delivery as an approved retention disposition. Historical relationships remain resolvable.

An operations job alerts before opening if the realized form cannot be generated for a known required locale or accommodations profile. A stale gradebook binding blocks formal release. Rate-limited deadline workers schedule from an indexed attempt due time, claim short leases and retry without duplicate expiry. Do not rely on an open browser page to complete an expired exam.
