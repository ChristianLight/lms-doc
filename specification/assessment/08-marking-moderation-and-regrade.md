# 08. Marking, moderation, correction and appeals

Only approved ItemMarkRevisions contribute to academic evidence. Candidate submission, auto evaluation, provisional marker marks, adjudication, academic grade projection and learner feedback disclosure are distinct lifecycle events. The authoritative mark has exact accepted rubric and evaluator provenance, immutable evidence references and an auditable acceptance actor.

## Queue and evaluator processing

Submission creates one assessment task per marked AttemptItem and processing profile. The task's natural key includes attempt item, submission manifest digest, rule graph digest and requested evaluation purpose. Use one durable EvaluationRun per natural key and run revision, with separate execution count, lease holder, lease expiry, completed result digest and last safe error. The scheduler transitions queued→leased→scored, manual_review, retry_wait or failed. Claim uses compare-and-swap and a short lease token. A worker must return the active lease token and matching input digest. If lease A expires and worker B claims the job, late result A is rejected and audited. A retry of an already accepted identical result yields the stored state. A mismatched repeat after acceptance opens an incident without overwriting the accepted result.

Worker leases default to 30 seconds, extend only while active, and time out at the profile's maximum wall-clock budget. Retry infrastructure faults five times with backoff 10, 30, 90, 270 and 810 seconds and bounded jitter. Individual learner answers never enter error logs. Terminal configuration errors route to operations. Unsupported or indeterminate but valid input routes to the marker where enabled. An evaluation run is an execution fact. A registered automatic acceptance service may accept approved deterministic results through a narrower permission than the worker. Human approval and independent moderation follow their own capabilities. ItemMarkRevision is a durable judgment and may combine approved evaluator results and authorized human rubric criteria.

For an item with maxima [2,3,5], deterministic values [1.5,2,4] produce 7.5/10. If the third rule is retrying, keep the item pending with accepted provisional 3.5/5 from complete criteria. Do not display or export 3.5/10 as official. A technical failure creates a review case, not a zero. Store canonical decimal at up to six fractional places. Apply rounding at the configured boundary. Document bonus or negative marks in the published slot profile and require compatible grade binding.

## Rubric marking

A rubric has stable criteria and a typed scoring mode. Level criteria have immutable keys, inclusive point intervals or single points, descriptors and allowed evidence tags. Free-point criteria declare step, maximum and require a comment when grading outside model exemplars. Sum criterion scores to raw item points after validating each. Do not calculate a score from a categorical descriptor unless its rubric version maps the descriptor to a numeric value. Rubric access controls determine whether candidates see descriptors, marker examples, model solution or review comments.

The marking assignment queue is scoped by offering, authorized section and question/subject capability. Unassigned eligible markers may claim available work by CAS where allowed. Blind assignments use per-delivery pseudonyms and hide learner roster attributes from reviewers. Blind attachments require a sanitized review projection. Marker sees prompt, resolved variant, evidence, approved rubric, previous marks only when authorized and comment fields divided into private and candidate-facing. Save the private draft head with ETag and append an attributed MarkDraftRevision for review and reassignment audit. Submit an immutable mark revision and release the assignment lock. Marker reassignment records old owner, new owner and reason. A departed marker's unfinished draft is retained but another marker cannot submit it as their own.

Double marking assigns independent tasks to two distinct eligible Principals. Do not show first score to second until both finalize. Default moderation triggers when absolute difference is more than 10% of item maximum, when level disagreement occurs on an essential criterion, or when one marker requests review. Where agreement is within threshold and the published policy uses mean, average criterion scores with approved rounding. A moderator can choose one exact mark, propose an adjudicated rubric score, or request third marking. Record cited inputs, reasoning and authorization. Example scores 7 and 9 on a 10-point item differ by 2, exceed threshold 1, and require adjudication. A moderator accepting 8 must justify it per criterion. A numeric override is a separate accepted mark revision. Keep original marker scores visible to authorized audit users.

## Attempt aggregate and evidence

When every required item has an accepted mark or a permitted explicit disposition, compute the attempt raw earned sum and sum of realized maxima. Normalize per slot where configured using exact rational scores and round once at declared boundaries. Preserve source mark IDs, actual maxima, total normalized points, partial/unresolved count and scoring digest. Default requires zero unresolved criteria before emitting earned GradeEvidence. A completed ungraded survey can emit completion without GradeEvidence. An accepted excuse or void emits corresponding evidence status and no numeric points. Team evidence emits one revision per eligible enrollment using deterministic member order and a separate reviewed adjustment where approved.

The results queue displays marked, waiting for moderation, awaiting disclosure, available for review, or corrected. Feedback policy independently releases answer visibility, marks, criterion detail, candidate-facing comments and model solutions. The default formal exam holds all except receipt until close and authorized release. Correct answers or explanations cannot be inferred from activity feed, embedded data, cached results or earlier practice feedback that uses a protected shared exam bank. Staff can release feedback only against explicit accepted revisions. A revised mark after disclosure requires fresh approval where policy holds corrected values.

## Regrade batch

1. Authorized assessment owner chooses delivery, source and replacement scoring revision, effective case population and reason. Capture approved fields that may be remapped. Regrade uses the original sealed evidence and actual variant, never a new random form or new learner answer.
2. In dry run, materialize a stable candidate/evidence manifest. For each binding check response family, source key, normalized value shape, expected evaluator profile, max scale and rubric semantics. Compatible key correction can recompute. Retyped or removed interaction routes to manual disposition. Scope changed max scores to a specifically reviewed correction plan. Record exact old/new points and status, incompatibility reason, effect on approved policy and required academic review.
3. Approver different from requester for formal exams approves exact dry-run digest, scope, decrease policy, treatment of overrides and deadline. The default preserves approved manual overrides and prevents automatically lowering already disclosed marks. Staff can authorize lowering under recorded institutional procedure and appropriate notification.
4. Enqueue deterministic per-attempt operations. Each checks original accepted mark head equals planned source revision. If a concurrent marker correction advanced it, mark that case conflicted and require a fresh dry-run or authorized exclusion. Each case can retry from its durable natural key. Write new accepted mark and GradeEvidence revisions using source lineage and emit revision event after commit.
5. Reconcile planned, updated, skipped, conflicted and failed counts. A stopped job preserves completed revisions. Abort prevents additional cases and requires an approved new corrective job to supersede already accepted records. Academic records decides whether and when to apply the evidence to a published result or report card. Never move the learner release pointer automatically.

For 60 delivered attempts, a changed choice answer yields 9 higher, 6 lower and 45 unchanged. Two of the six lower already have manual overrides. An approved preserve-overrides, no-decrease policy writes nine higher revisions, records two overridden exclusions and four decrease exclusions, and does not rewrite unchanged records. If one of the nine has a concurrent adjudication since the preview, it enters conflict, leaving eight changed until reconciled.

## Mark appeals

A learner challenges disclosed assessment evidence through an AssessmentAppeal. Include attempt and disclosed mark reference, requested issue classification, reason and optional evidence. The default filing window is 14 calendar days after disclosure in institution time zone. Provide staff scope for extension and accessibility alternatives. Keep at most one active appeal per candidate and mark revision. A scope-authorized coordinator can decline out-of-window or duplicate submissions with a reason. Reviewers must differ from original decision-maker on formal exams where feasible. A conflict of interest reassigns the case. Accepted decision writes new mark/evidence only through the same acceptance commands. Dismissed or unchanged decision retains reason and feedback. Close, withdraw, extend and escalate are explicit states. A subsequent challenge to official grade aggregation is routed to academic records rather than changing assessment marks. Candidate sees only the allowed decision and reasons.

Review and appeals retention can exceed draft response retention. Put holds on every referenced submission, mark and review decision while an appeal or incident is active. A formal institution decision controls purge and redaction. Anonymous peer reviewer identity stays protected from ordinary appeals users.
